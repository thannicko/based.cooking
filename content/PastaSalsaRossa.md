Pasta salsa rossa.

- ⏲️ Prep time: 30 min
- 🍳 Cook time: 30 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 4

## Ingredients

Toppings to go with sauce:
- Vegetables of choice (courgettes, paprika, sperziebonen)
- Protein of choice (kipstukjes, oesterzwammen)
- Parmesan cheese (powder or grated)

Sauce ingredients:
- Olive oil
- Butter
- 1x Onion
- 3x Garlic cloves
- 500g Roma tomatoes
- 400g Tomaten gepeld blikjes
- 125ml Creme fraiche

Herbs powder mix (credits: [Front Range Fed](https://www.frontrangefed.com/easy-homemade-spaghetti-sauce-seasoning-recipe/))
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1.5 tablespoon oregano
- 3 tablespoon basil
- 1 tablespoon rosemary
- 1 teaspoon thyme
- 2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon sage
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1 pinch fennel seeds (finely ground)

## Directions

Making toppings:
1. Heat olive oil in pan with medium heat.
2. Add vegetables of choice.
3. When cooked, set vegetables aside.
4. Repeat this to cook protein of choice, also set aside.

Making sauce:
1. Cut up fresh tomatoes.
2. Heat olive oil and butter in pan with medium heat.
3. Add onion and sauté until lightly golden.
4. Add garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes.
5. Add fresh tomatoes, cook for 10 minutes over high heat. 
6. Add canned tomatoes, reduce heat to medium-low.
7. Simmer for 5-10 minutes more. While it's simmering, assemble the herbs mix.
8. Add herbs mix and creme fraiche. Stir well.
9. Turn off fire.

Serve sauce with pasta, the toppings prepared, and Parmesan cheese. Garnish with fresh basil if available.
